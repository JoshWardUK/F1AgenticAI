Silverstone 2026 Race Report

Qualifying summary

Silverstone’s 2026 British Grand Prix qualifying was led by Andrea Kimi Antonelli for Mercedes with a pole time of 1:28.111. Charles Leclerc was second for Ferrari on 1:28.286, Lewis Hamilton third for Ferrari on 1:28.458, and George Russell fourth for Mercedes on 1:28.481. Isack Hadjar took fifth for Red Bull on 1:28.746, with Lando Norris sixth for McLaren and Max Verstappen seventh for Red Bull. Oscar Piastri qualified eighth for McLaren. The remaining top ten were Arvid Lindblad in ninth and Liam Lawson tenth, both for RB F1 Team.

The rest of the field was Gabriel Bortoleto 11th, Pierre Gasly 12th, Nico Hülkenberg 13th, Oliver Bearman 14th, Carlos Sainz 15th, Alexander Albon 16th, Esteban Ocon 17th, Valtteri Bottas 18th, Franco Colapinto 19th, Sergio Pérez 20th, Lance Stroll 21st, and Fernando Alonso 22nd.
