# Norris tops final-lap timing order after Piastri’s long spell in front at Silverstone

Lando Norris headed the recorded order on the last of 52 laps at the 2025 British Grand Prix, after Oscar Piastri led from lap eight to lap 43 and Nico Hülkenberg rose from 19th in qualifying to third in the closing timing records.

Silverstone’s round 12, held on 6 July, produced a clear progression at the front: Max Verstappen led the opening seven laps, Piastri headed the next 36, and Norris occupied first place from lap 44 onwards.

This account follows the retrieved qualifying results and complete lap-timing feed. The lap feed is not an independently verified final classification, and changes in recorded position do not establish overtakes or explain how those changes occurred. No weather, race-control, pit-stop or penalty records were retrieved.

Fernando Alonso took ninth for Aston Martin in 1:25.621, while Alpine’s Pierre Gasly completed the top ten with 1:25.785.

## Sources and data limitations

The race identity and date come from the retrieved 2025 calendar. Qualifying and lap timings were retrieved from the Jolpica F1 API and saved as `silverstone_2025_qualifying.csv` and `silverstone_2025_lap_timings.csv`. All 20 qualifying entries and all 822 available driver timings across 52 laps were retained. The lap tool fetched nine pages and reported the retrieval complete; both saved CSVs were subsequently read in full.

The lap CSV uses one row per lap, with each position column containing the recorded driver ID and lap time. Empty cells mean no timing was supplied for that position and lap. Lawson and Colapinto have no lap timings in the feed; Bortoleto’s records end at lap three, Hadjar’s at lap 17, Antonelli’s at lap 23 and Tsunoda’s at lap 51. These absences do not, by themselves, establish retirement causes or race status.

An initial qualifying-tool response incorrectly contained a page of lap data. A repeat request returned all 20 actual qualifying results; the complete lap retrieval also contains all timings from that initial response. No qualifying results were inferred from the incorrect response.
